# NTT Address Generator Simulation Report

## Module Name

`ntt_addr_gen`

## Purpose

Verify the Kyber forward and inverse NTT butterfly address schedule generated by `rtl/ntt/ntt_addr_gen.v`.

## Files Tested

- RTL: `rtl/ntt/ntt_addr_gen.v`
- Testbench: `tb/unit/tb_ntt_addr_gen.v`
- Run script: `sim/scripts/run_ntt_addr_gen.sh`
- Log: `sim/logs/ntt_addr_gen_sim.log`
- Waveform: `sim/waves/ntt_addr_gen.vcd`
- Compiled output: `sim/outputs/tb_ntt_addr_gen.out`

## Test Strategy

The testbench applies reset, pulses `start` for one clock, then checks every valid output against a behavioral model of the nested loops from `kyber768/ntt.c`.

Checked every valid cycle:

- `len_out`
- `addr_a`
- `addr_b`
- `zeta_addr`

Also checked:

- total valid cycles
- one-cycle `done` pulse after the final valid schedule
- `valid` low after `done`
- `busy` low after `done`
